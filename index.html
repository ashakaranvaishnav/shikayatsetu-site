About and disclaimer

ShikayatSetu.in is an independent directory that helps people find the official government website for their complaint. It is not affiliated with the Government of India or any state government. We do not collect, file or track complaints, and we never ask for OTPs, passwords or fees. Always check that the address in your browser matches the official website shown on the card.
